Frequently Asked Questions

Is the free plan really free?

Yes! The free plan has no time limit and includes all basic features including three difficulty modes and a local leaderboard.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. Cancel your subscription at any time — no questions asked, no lock-in period.

How is the score calculated?

Score = Max Range − Number of Attempts. For example, on Medium (1–100) with 5 guesses your score is 95. Fewer guesses = higher score!

Is there a mobile app?

The web app is fully responsive and works perfectly on all mobile browsers. Native iOS and Android apps are coming soon!

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept all major credit/debit cards via Stripe, and PayPal. All payments are processed securely.

What is Extreme mode?

Extreme mode expands the range to 1–1000, dramatically increasing the challenge. It's available exclusively to Pro and Enterprise subscribers.
